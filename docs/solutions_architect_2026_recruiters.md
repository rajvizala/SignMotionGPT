## 4. Supporting Context: The AWS TechU / New Grad SA Pipeline

The Solutions Architect - 2026 (US) role (Job ID: 3204552) is part of Amazon's accelerated career development program for new graduates. Internally this feeds into the **AWS TechU program** - a 12-month accelerated development program with 6 months cohort training + 6 months on-the-job training, targeting grads with limited professional experience.

Key program facts:
- Graduation window: May 2024 - April 2026
- Prepares candidates for AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate certification
- Participants placed on teams like Worldwide Public Sector Central SA, Commercial SA, or specialized vertical teams (FinServ, Healthcare, etc.)
- The Central SA program has trained 275+ Solutions Architects and was specifically designed to hire women and underrepresented early-career talent
